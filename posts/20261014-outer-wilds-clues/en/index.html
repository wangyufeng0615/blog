What happens in one loop

Who points you where

Timber Hearth village Hal, Hornfels and the others

  • → Gabbro’s Island

    Hal says Gabbro went back to Giant’s Deep to try to learn more about the Nomai statue in the observatory.

  • → Esker’s Camp

    Sounds like Esker is still stationed on the Attlerock. They’ve been there by themself for a while.

  • → Eye Signal Locator

    I hear there are Nomai ruins somewhere on the Attlerock. No one knows what they are or why they were built. The Nomai text in the observatory talks about calibrating some sort of device on the Attlerock.

Esker the Attlerock

  • → Dark Bramble Seed

    Esker's signalscope log reports harmonica music coming from somewhere on Timber Hearth. They claim it sounds just like Feldspar's harmonica, but Feldspar disappeared in space ages ago.

  • → Lunar Lookout

    Esker says the Attlerock’s north pole (marked in red on my minimap) is a great spot to listen for the other travelers’ music with a signalscope.

Chert Ember Twin

  • → Escape Pod 2

    Chert saw a Nomai shipwreck shooting a beam of light into the sky somewhere on Ember Twin's southern hemisphere.

  • → The Sunless City

    Chert thinks the lack of surface ruins means the Nomai must have lived somewhere underground.

Riebeck Brittle Hollow

  • → Southern Observatory

    Riebeck says you can't get into the observatory from the surface (they tried), but there's probably a path to it beneath the crust somewhere.

Gabbro Giant’s Deep

  • → Ocean Depths

    Gabbro says there’s a strong current beneath the surface of the ocean that prevents anything from sinking below it.

  • → Statue Island

    One of the islands on Giant’s Deep has a Nomai statue on the beach just like the one Gabbro brought back to Timber Hearth. Statue Island (as Gabbro calls it) is the one with two islands connected by a natural rock arch.

  • → Orbital Probe Cannon

    Gabbro sees a bright flash in the sky at the start of every loop. Something must be happening in orbit around Giant’s Deep.

Feldspar Dark Bramble

  • → Frozen Jellyfish

    Feldspar hinted that a secret to reaching the core of Giant's Deep lies at the end of the hollow vine they crashed their ship into. To find Feldspar's ship, I'll want to go to the tail end of the anglerfish skeleton and look for a flickering light in the fog.

Tools and abilities

Launch codes Hornfels at the observatory

  • Ride the lift up the launch tower
  • Unlocks dozing at campfires

Little Scout Launcher on your suit

  • A photo pins a quantum object in place
  • Peek through small seeds
  • Its tracker shows it in two places at once
  • Its camera sees ghost matter
  • Light up the fossil’s mouth

Signalscope Starts with the Travelers frequency; learns others near their sources

  • Travelers: follow the harmonica to Feldspar
  • Distress Beacon: appears after your first escape pod, leads to Escape Pod 3
  • Quantum Fluctuations: shards and the Quantum Moon
  • Find the travelers’ instruments at the Eye

Translator Standard gear

  • Read Nomai spiral text and recordings — nearly every clue depends on it

Flashlight Your suit

  • Turn it off for total darkness and you become entangled
  • Lights out in the shrine to move with the moon

Suit and jetpack By the launch tower

  • Jump between misaligned gravity lifts
  • Too weak for Giant’s Deep’s gravity on high ledges

Ship and ship log Timber Hearth launch tower

  • Cut the engines and drift past anglerfish
  • The log records every clue and rumor; the question marks in rumor mode are places you haven’t been

Campfire On every planet

  • Doze to skip time, e.g. until the sand drains
  • Meditate to end the loop early: from the third loop, ask Gabbro how they stay so calm
  • Roast marshmallows to heal

Nomai devices All over the system

  • Warp pads: wait for the target overhead
  • Gravity cannons: recall a shuttle
  • Projection pools: see far-off places, like the sunken tracking module

Endings

The Eye of the Universe Main ending

  1. Put the project’s core in the Vessel, enter the coordinates, warp
  2. Walk from the north pole to the south, drop into the crater in the quantum storm, arrive at a quantum observatory
  3. Light a campfire in the glade, find each traveler’s instrument with the signalscope, and play together
  4. If you met Solanum she joins too, and the post-credits shot gains a new campfire with alien campers
But I think the rules are about to change.

Stay at the sixth location Hidden ending

  1. Take the project’s core; the loop stops
  2. Land on the Quantum Moon, go to its sixth location, wait for the supernova
HOW LONG HAVE YOU BEEN HERE? MINUTES? YEARS? YOU ARE UNSURE BUT IT SEEMS YOUR JOURNEY HAS REACHED ITS END.

At launch this ending showed only “813” — the line’s ID in the game text — so players called it the 813 ending until patch 1.0.2.

Adrift Hidden ending

  1. Take the project’s core
  2. Fly far enough from the Sun to outrun the supernova
NOW BEYOND THE REACH OF THE SUPERNOVA, YOU DRIFT THROUGH SPACE UNTIL YOUR SHIP'S RESOURCES ARE FINALLY DEPLETED

Breaking spacetime Hidden ending

  1. In the High Energy Lab, fit a black hole core and a white hole core and fire your scout into the black hole
  2. After it comes out of the white hole but before it goes in, pull a core: now two scouts exist
YOU DESTROYED THE FABRIC OF SPACETIME

The credits then race by to a kazoo cover of the theme.

Meeting yourself Hidden ending

  1. As the supernova hits, jump into the black hole that opens at the center of the Ash Twin Project
  2. Next loop another you is waiting in the project; skip the jump this time and spacetime breaks
Are you me from the last loop?

Added in patch 1.0.4.

Game over Death

  1. Die before the statue pairs with you, and the game starts over
  2. Die after taking the project’s core, and you don’t wake up
YOU ARE DEAD

FAQ

What do you need to reach the ending of Outer Wilds?
Three things: the Eye of the Universe’s coordinates (in the Probe Tracking Module at the core of Giant’s Deep), the Nomai Vessel (stuck deep in Dark Bramble), and the advanced warp core (in the central chamber of the Ash Twin Project). Put the core in the Vessel, enter the coordinates on the bridge, and warp to the Eye. Taking the core stops the time loop, so do it last.
Where is the Vessel in Dark Bramble and how do you get in?
Use the signalscope’s distress frequency to find Escape Pod 3 and follow the survivors’ lights to the Nomai Grave. The seed there only fits your scout; once inside, the tracker shows the scout in two places, and the other one is an unlit, ship-sized seed in the anglerfish nest. Slip past the anglerfish in the nest and take that seed to the Vessel.
How do you get past the anglerfish in Dark Bramble?
Anglerfish are blind and hunt by sound, so cut your engines and drift. The clue is the anglerfish fossil on Ember Twin, where Nomai children played a game in which the “anglerfish” wore a blindfold. You leave each seed at a fixed speed and heading, so simply drifting works well.
How do you get below Giant’s Deep’s current and through the electric field?
The intended way is to ride a counterclockwise cyclone down; the clue is the cyclone model in Brittle Hollow’s Southern Observatory. A very fast dive or a falling island also works. The electric field around the core can only be crossed inside a jellyfish; Feldspar’s notes in Dark Bramble say their rubbery skin insulates them.
How do you get into the Ash Twin Project?
Its shell has no physical entrance, so you have to warp. The White Hole Station teaches how warp pads work; the tower designs in the High Energy Lab show where each tower goes; the Black Hole Forge says the Hourglass Twins share one alignment point between them. Put together: one tower on Ash Twin’s equator has its receiver at the planet’s center — stand on the pad inside it and wait for Ember Twin to pass overhead. The tower is buried in sand until about 7:50 into the loop.
How do you get into the Sunless City and the High Energy Lab?
The Sunless City is beneath Ember Twin: follow the wall markers from the caves under Escape Pod 2, or drop through a hidden hole above the gravity cannon, before the sand fills it. The High Energy Lab’s front door is locked from inside; take the trail along the power cable from the city’s lowest district, early enough that the first half isn’t flooded and late enough that sand has buried the cacti on the second half.
How do you reach the Black Hole Forge and the Southern Observatory?
The Forge hangs below the Hanging City: raise it from the control room in the Meltwater District, then warp to the Forge District from the Brittle Hollow tower on Ash Twin (the shaft is broken). The Southern Observatory’s surface door is broken; go under the crust, either from the gravity cannon across a chain of gravity lifts or from the base of the Tower of Quantum Knowledge.
How do you reach the Quantum Moon’s sixth location?
You need all three quantum rules: imaging (Tower of Quantum Trials on Giant’s Deep), entanglement (Lakebed Cave on Ember Twin), and the sixth location (Tower of Quantum Knowledge on Brittle Hollow: the shrine must be at the moon’s north pole). Shut yourself in the shrine in the dark to travel with it; get it to the north pole and use it once more to meet Solanum.
What happened to the Nomai?
After the Sun Station failed, the Nomai went to study a newly arrived comet, the Interloper. Its core held ghost matter under extreme pressure; when it ruptured, the ghost matter covered the solar system almost instantly. It is invisible to the eye but shows up on camera.
